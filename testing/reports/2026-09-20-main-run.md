Group 2 (main tier) of the tiered suite on the "Pray Smoke iPhone 17 Pro"
simulator. Two full passes plus targeted re-runs; the tier membership changed
during the day as interdependent flows were moved into dedicated suites
(lock, jrn, background) — see `2026-09-20-critical-run.md` for the shared

## Pass 1: 11 passed / 30 failed (41 flows)

Two systemic effects dominated:

   15 flows after it failed on the lock screen (all `clearState` does not

   settings in the "Данные для ИИ" BottomSheet, removed settings captions,

## Pass 2: 22 passed / 13 failed (35 flows)

- 3 flows wait for the stub fixture "Псалом 22:1–6" → moved to the stub phase

  scripture-highlight.

  the live server (passage count/order varies; scr-002 expects the same

  stub phase.

  not guarantee → moved into `ios-jrn-suite.yaml` (7 ordered flows, all green).
- music-timer-finish: six ~16 s Maestro taps could not burn a 5-minute timer
  down; now opens the timer panel (`session-timer-button`) and waits for the
  natural expiry (timeout 360 s).

  (prompt prefetch); assert removed, presence of a loaded question is checked
  via the "Ответить" dock instead.
- jrn-005-prayer-a: the recordings sheet must be closed explicitly ("Закрыть
  записи") before the finish step.
- Maestro text matching is full-string for accessibility labels: "Слушать"
  does not match the label "Слушать отрывок"; audio checks now use the testID
  `scripture-audio-button`.
