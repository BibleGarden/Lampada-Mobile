# Local background music

Date of the check: 23 August 2026.

## What was implemented

- fifteen local CC0 tracks of about 45 minutes in total;
- a looping foreground playlist with no network requests;
- an on/off button and a state indicator on the prayer screen;
- the state chip placed with its own gap above the timer ring;
- a pause when the app goes to the background, during a voice recording and
  while listening to a draft;
- a stop when the session finishes;
- the authors, the sources, the licenses and the checksums recorded in
  `assets/audio/README.md`.

## The finish crash that was fixed

The original Release build crashed twice on the transition to the reflection with
`ERR_NATIVE_SHARED_OBJECT_NOT_FOUND`: the cleanup called `pause()` after
`useAudioPlaylist` had already released the native shared object. The cleanup no
longer touches the playlist; the normal transition stops it before
`router.replace` and waits for the global audio session to be released.

After the fix both paths were reproduced - the timer reaching zero and finishing
early. There are no new `.ips` files, `RCTFatal`, `SIGABRT` or
`ERR_NATIVE_SHARED_OBJECT_NOT_FOUND` since the retests began.

## The manual check on a device

The simulator confirms the build and the UI, but it does not replace listening.
On a physical iPhone one has to:

1. install the Release build with `npm run iphone`;
2. start a prayer and press the note button - the music should play quietly with
   no network;
3. background the app and come back - the music should stop in the background and
   continue after the return;
4. with the music on, record a short voice answer and listen to it - the music
   should stop before the recording and be absent from the saved audio;
5. finish the prayer - the music must not continue on the reflection.

Until that run the listening part counts as not manually verified.

## Extending the catalogue to 15 tracks

Another 11 CC0 pieces were added. Loops that were too short, at 17-34 seconds,
and tracks with an anxious, horror or distinctly game-like character were
excluded during the selection. The total length of the catalogue is about 45
minutes and the MP3s take 31.6 MB.

After the extension `npm run typecheck`, 16/16 unit tests, Expo Doctor 21/21 and
`expo export --platform ios` all passed. The export listed all 15 MP3s and took
about 43 MB in total. A clean native Release build was called off by the project
owner as unnecessary; the extended catalogue needs a listening retest at the next
installation through `npm run iphone`.
