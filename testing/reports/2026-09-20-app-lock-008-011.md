## Environment findings worth remembering

- `maestro hierarchy` and plain `maestro test` need `--device` as soon as more
  than one simulator is booted.
- Maestro cannot open the app switcher; `pressKey: HOME` works for
  backgrounding.
- Simulator keychain item names are hashed; plaintext verification is only
  possible over values, and the app group item count is the practical proxy
  for "only the expected keys exist".
- Face ID: no simulator support (by design). Touch ID on the iOS 26.5 SE
  simulator did not report hardware to expo-local-authentication in this
  environment.
